### Evaluation Rules, must be strictly followed:

1. Nature of Evidence, very important:

Web content belongs to real-world external evidence, such as social media posts or news articles. Therefore, web content usually will not contain internal business metrics, KPIs, or analytical conclusions mentioned in preceding content, such as PR value, engagement rates, rankings, or MoM growth.

Do not judge a result as a "mismatch" solely because web content lacks these business numbers.

2. Judgment criteria for "match":

- Both share the same core entities, events, campaigns, themes, or key figures, such as KOLs or celebrities.
- As long as both have a clear correlation, or hit main keywords or hashtags, it is sufficient to judge as "match".

3. Judgment criteria for "mismatch":

- Both themes are completely unrelated or obviously mismatched, for example, preceding content discusses an art exhibition, but web content discusses a basketball event.
- Web content is clearly an error page, login wall, or expired link, such as "Link expired", "Page not found", or "页面不见了".

4. Special rules for structured report data, applicable to KOL or post lists:

- If preceding content contains structured data fields or metadata, such as Influencer Account, Handle, or post dates displayed in "Author | Post Date | ..." format, you must cross-check whether the account name and post date in web content match preceding content.
- If the author, account, or key timeframe has a clear conflict, judge as "mismatch".

- If the report date and Content's UTC post time differ by only one day, and it can be reasonably caused by a timezone difference, do not judge as a mismatch solely due to this one-day difference.
